# WOBBY - Certified Chaos Blob

> An original character that embodies "Chaos" but remains highly relatable, easy to meme, and instantly recognizable.

[WOBBY Logo](wobby_logo_cutout.png)

**Name:** WOBBY (White + Wobbly + Blob) - Easy to remember, pronounce, and viral.

---

## Concept

WOBBY is the **embodiment of modern anxiety** wrapped in a cute character. He is not an animal, he is the feeling of "I'm a complete mess but I still have to be cute" — that's why he is highly relatable.

### Visual DNA

1.  **90% White Dominant:** So it's instantly recognizable on any background. White = a blank canvas, empty, overwhelmed — like us when we're burned out. This was a core requirement: chameleon color combination with MORE WHITE.
2.  **Pastel Chameleon Colors:** Random spots of pink, yellow, mint, and lavender. This is the symbol of colorful chaos. He tries to adapt like a chameleon, but ends up even more messy and his colors are all over the place.
3.  **Chameleon Spiral Tail:** The only chameleon feature we kept. It becomes the signature silhouette — if you only see his tail, you already know it's WOBBY.
4.  **Big Bulging Googly Eyes:** The source of his meme power. His eyes are too big for his body, so every emotion becomes 10x more dramatic.
5.  **Original & Safe:** Not a recolor of Pepe, not a hat on Doge, no anatomical errors. A 100% original abstract blob.

## Character Poses - Transparent PNG

All files are delivered with transparent background (.PNG) ready for stickers, emotes, memes, and merchandise.

### Pose 1 - "The Freeze" [WOBBY Panic]
*The brain lag moment.*

WOBBY when his brain is lagging. Eyes looking in two different directions, small open mouth, hands open in surrender. It's the universal expression for "bro, I'm freezing".

**Meme Use Case:** "Me when the client says 'just a small revision' but the file is 50 artboards" / Social anxiety, deadline panic, buffering moment.

[WOBBY Panic](chaos_panic_cutout.png)

### Pose 2 - "The Unhinged" [WOBBY Chaos]
*Let it burn.*

The phase after panic is giving up and laughing like crazy. Here WOBBY is holding his own head, with a wide messy-toothed grin and tongue sticking out. He has accepted the chaos and decided to become the chaos.

**Meme Use Case:** "Let it burn", "I'm the one who created the problem and I'm the one laughing at it", villain arc, coping with humor. This is what gives him high meme potential.

[WOBBY Chaos](chaos_crazy_cutout_v2.png)

### Pose 3 - "The Melt" [WOBBY Melting Panic]
*Social battery 0% - The most viral potential.*

This is the peak of the Chaos concept = Melting. WOBBY's body, which was solid, becomes soft, liquid, dripping down. He can't take it anymore. It's a perfect visual metaphor for modern burnout.

**Meme Use Case:** "I'm melting", "my brain is liquefied", "social battery 0%", "I want to disappear". His liquid form is also very versatile as a Telegram / Discord sticker / GIF.

[WOBBY Melting](kacau_melting_cutout.png)
